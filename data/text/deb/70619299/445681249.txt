This letter, together with the attached Schedule(s) and General Terms and Conditions, which form 
part of and are incorporated by reference herein, sets forth the agreement (“Agreement”) between 
AlixPartners, LLP (“AlixPartners”), and Latham & Watkins LLP (the “Firm”) for the engagement of

indirect subsidiaries (collectively, the “Company”). All defined terms shall have the meanings 
ascribed to them in this letter and in the attached Schedule(s) and General Terms and Conditions. 
The Firm, the Company and AlixPartners are each a “Party,” as the context requires, and together 
the “Parties.”

Objectives and Tasks

The responsibilities of AlixPartners may include, but not be limited to, assisting the Company and 
its other advisors with the following:

•
Develop and refine its rolling 13-week cash receipts and disbursements forecast;

•
Work with the Company to identify and implement near-term liquidity enhancement 
initiatives;

•
Design and implement a restructuring strategy to maximize enterprise value and creditor 
recovery, taking into account the unique interests of all constituencies, including 
contingency planning and wind down alternatives;

•
Develop any revised business plan that maybe needed, and such other related forecasts as 
may be required by lenders in connection with negotiations or by the Company for other 
corporate purposes;

•
Communications and/or negotiations with outside parties including the Company’s 
stakeholders and potential acquirers of Company assets and advisors to the foregoing;

•
Negotiate any financing, including DIP and exit financing facilities, in conjunction with a 
plan of reorganization, sale in chapter 11 and the overall restructuring; and

•
Such other matters as may be requested that fall within AlixPartners’ expertise and that 
are mutually agreeable.

It is understood that the services being rendered by AlixPartners pursuant to this Agreement are 
being performed at the request of the Company or the Firm, on behalf of the Company. Each of 
the parties hereto acknowledges, without assuming any responsibility or obligation therefor, that 
it is the Firm’s intention to assert that certain communications and correspondence between 
AlixPartners and the Firm, on behalf of the Company, in connection with the subject matter of 
this engagement, and certain work product and analysis prepared by AlixPartners for the 
Company in connection with said subject matter, is confidential and intended to be considered in 
preparation for or in anticipation of litigation over the potential restructuring of the Company and, 
accordingly, subject to any and all applicable privileges, immunities and protections, including, 
but not limited to, the attorney-client privilege or work-product doctrine or other applicable 
privilege doctrines available under applicable law.

Fees and Expenses

1. Fees: AlixPartners’ fees will be based on the hours spent by AlixPartners personnel at 
AlixPartners’ hourly rates, which are:

Partner / Partner & Managing Director
USD 1,225 – USD 1,540

Senior Vice President / Director
USD 850 – USD 1,150

Vice President/Consultant/Analyst
USD 250 – USD 835

AlixPartners generally reviews and revises its billing rates semi-annually.

AlixPartners’ total fees include any retainer, break fee, or success fee payable hereunder, if 
any (together, the “Fees”). In the event of a Chapter 11 filing, the Fees shall also include the 
Case Management Platform usage fees set forth in the Bankruptcy Related Matters Section of 
the General Terms and Conditions.

2.
Expenses: In addition to the Fees set forth in this Schedule, the Company shall pay 
directly, or reimburse AlixPartners upon receipt of periodic billings, for all reasonable out-of-
pocket expenses incurred in connection with this engagement, such as travel, lodging and 
meals.

3. Retainer:  Upon execution of the Agreement, the Company will pay AlixPartners a retainer of 
USD 250,000. In the event of a Chapter 11 filing, any balance of the retainer will be held as 
an evergreen retainer, pending approval of the Bankruptcy Court.

4. Payment: AlixPartners will submit invoices no less frequently than bi-weekly for services 
rendered and expenses incurred. All invoices shall be due and payable immediately upon 
receipt. In the event of a Chapter 11 filing, AlixPartners will submit invoices in the manner 
required by the Bankruptcy Court. All invoices will be due and payable immediately upon 
receipt, subject to Bankruptcy Court approval, as applicable.
