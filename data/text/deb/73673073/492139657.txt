BASIS FOR RELIEF

10. 
Pursuant to Local Rule 9006-1(d), “[r]eply papers . . . may be filed and, if filed,

shall be served so as to be received by 4:00 p.m. prevailing Eastern Time the day prior to the

deadline for filing the agenda.”  The Debtor was required to file the agenda for the Hearing on or

before September 1, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. (ET).  Accordingly, the Debtor’s deadline to file a reply

to the Objections was August 31, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. (ET) (the “Reply Deadline”).  Given the

timing, volume, and complexity of the Objections, the Debtor was unable to fully evaluate and

prepare responses to the numerous factual and legal issues raised in the Objections by the Reply

Deadline.

11. 
In addition to filing the Objections, the Quantum Parties have actively litigated

multiple contested matters in the Chapter 11 Case, including filing their Motion of Omnis Fuel

Technologies, LLC d/b/a Omnis Energy and Quantum Pleasants, LLC for Entry of an Order (I)

Dismissing the Debtor’s Chapter 11 Case, or (II) in the Alternative, Directing the Appointment of

a Chapter 11 Trustee, and (III) Vacating the Interim Cash Collateral Order [Docket No. 93],

serving extensive discovery requests on the Debtor and the Prepetition Secured Parties, and

seeking deposition testimony from the Debtor’s Chief Executive Officer.  As a result, the issues

raised by the Underlying Pleadings and the Objections have become substantially more complex.

12. 
Accordingly, rather than filing multiple piecemeal responses, the Debtor prepared

a single omnibus reply that comprehensively addresses the various factual and legal arguments

asserted by the Objecting Parties and explains why the relief requested in the Underlying Pleadings

should be granted.

13. 
By this Motion, the Debtor respectfully requests leave and permission to file the

Reply after the Reply Deadline.  The Debtor submits that the Reply will assist the Court in its
