8.

efforts.  Since May 2024, independent restructuring professionals have managed the Company 
through an extensive wind-down, conducted without a chapter 11 filing at the Debtor level 
(although, as described below, certain subsidiaries were placed into insolvency or administration

and headcount, disposed of or transitioned numerous businesses and investments, reduced or 
resolved significant secured indebtedness, and preserved assets that require additional time to 
monetize.
